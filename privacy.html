Privacy Policy

How M&M Cleaning Solutions collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: August 5, 2026

1. Who we are

M&M Cleaning Solutions (“we,” “us,” or “our”) provides residential, Airbnb, move in/out, deep cleaning, recurring, and event cleaning services in Barrie and surrounding areas.

Contact us at mmcleaningsolution26@gmail.com or call/text 204 922 1052.

2. Information we collect

We may collect information you choose to give us, including:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Service requested and message details
  • Reviews or comments you submit on our website

When you visit our website, standard technical data may also be processed by our hosting and form providers (such as browser type, device information, and approximate location) to help the site work securely.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to quote requests and messages
  • Schedule and provide cleaning services
  • Follow up about bookings, questions, or feedback
  • Improve our website and customer experience
  • Display reviews you choose to submit

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Contact form and email delivery

Our contact form is processed through Web3Forms so your message can be delivered to our business email. Information you submit in the form is used only to respond to your request and provide related service communication.

5. Reviews

If you leave a review on our website, the name, rating, and comment you provide may be shown publicly on the Reviews page. Please do not include private information you do not want displayed.

6. Cookies and preferences

Our site may store a simple preference in your browser (such as light/dark theme) so your choice is remembered on your next visit. You can clear this anytime in your browser settings.

7. How we share information

We may share information with trusted service providers that help us run the website (for example hosting and form/email delivery). They are only given what is needed to perform those services.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, customers, or the public.

8. Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for business communication, service records, or legal requirements, then delete or securely dispose of it when it is no longer needed.

9. Your choices

You may request to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete information we no longer need

Email mmcleaningsolution26@gmail.com and we will do our best to help.

10. Children’s privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the website after updates means you accept the revised policy.

12. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Reach out anytime:

  • Email: mmcleaningsolution26@gmail.com
  • Phone / Text: 204 922 1052
